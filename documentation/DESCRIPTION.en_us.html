Kuhmard (کوهمرد) is a Persian slab serif with its own Latin, made to sit beside Latin slab serifs in bilingual design. Its Persian letters follow the lettering of the 3rd Tehran International Film Festival (1974): square ends, heavy strokes and a level, architectural rhythm. The Latin is adapted from Rokkitt and set to the same letter height and stroke, so both scripts read as one design.

Kuhmard covers Persian, Arabic and Urdu letters and the Google Fonts Latin Core set. It is the first typeface from Azrast, a Persian type foundry. To contribute, see github.com/azrast-type/kuhmard.
